Who we are

A new take on everyday luxury.

In my twenties I had one simple obsession: finding a sweatshirt that felt genuinely comfortable, without ever making me feel like I had given up on looking put-together.

I couldn’t find it, so I decided to create it.

This startup was born in Puglia, surrounded by the authentic beauty, with a clear eye on innovation and modern design. We wanted to reimagine a piece usually seen as purely athletic. We reworked the fit, and chose only 100% high-quality Italian cotton—soft against the skin and made to hold its shape over time.

We create small, thoughtfully crafted drops designed to take you from morning meetings to sunset drinks. Because dressing comfortably should never mean dressing carelessly. Dress down, but stay chic.
